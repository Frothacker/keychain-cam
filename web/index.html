How it works

These keychain cameras (sold as "G6" or "mini 1080p", built on an HX‑Tech HX330X chip; this is the one I bought) turn into a normal USB webcam when you pick PC Camera. This page asks your browser for that webcam and does everything else on your own device: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari on a Mac, Windows PC or Chromebook, and in Chrome on most Android phones.
  • Not on iPhone. iOS doesn't let apps or websites use USB webcams. iPads with USB‑C can, in apps.
  • Offline: after one visit the page keeps working with no internet. In Chrome use Install in the address bar, or in Safari File → Add to Dock, to get it as its own app.
  • Photos are 1920×1080 JPEGs. For the full 2560×1440 photos, use the camera on its own and copy them off the card.
  • Sound: the camera's own mic is used by default. It's very quiet and crackly, so this page boosts it and cleans it up with a small AI noise remover. Try the filters on your own voice in the Sound Lab. Your computer's mic usually sounds better.

There's also a Mac app with auto‑import and time‑lapse, and notes on hacking the camera's firmware: github.com/Frothacker/keychain-cam.
