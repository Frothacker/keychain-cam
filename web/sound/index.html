Sound Lab

The keychain camera's mic is tiny, quiet and crackly. Record a short clip, let it loop, and flip filters on and off while you listen. When it sounds right, use those settings for your recordings.

What's going on

The camera mic picks up speech about 40 dB quieter than a normal mic, so everything has to be boosted a lot, and the boost makes its hiss and crackle loud too. Simple filters (cutting the top end, a gate) help a bit. The AI noise removers are the big step: they've learned what a voice sounds like and throw away the rest. Your clip never leaves your device.

Apple's own Voice Isolation (the one in FaceTime and Final Cut) is even stronger, but browsers can't use it. The Mac app can.
